# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Findings — Kolkata AQI

**Dataset:** Kolkata air quality observations (CPCB historical data).
**Time Coverage:** 2015-01-01 to 2015-01-05
**Total Observations:** 5

## Key Findings Supported by the Data

1. The average AQI over the period is 151.4.
2. The highest average AQI occurs in month 1 (average 151.4).
3. Seasonal analysis shows the following average AQI: Winter (151.4).
4. The strongest pollutant-AQI correlation is with pm2.5 (0.88).
5. The highest single-day AQI recorded was 160.0 on 2015-01-04.
